# Curriculum Blueprint

## Scope

The first release contains 108 lessons arranged into nine equal modules. The sequence moves from data literacy and descriptive methods through inference, regression, applied analytics, data science, data engineering, and advanced statistics.

| Module | Lessons | Primary outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Data & Statistics Foundations | 12 | Define data correctly, understand sampling, quality, and reproducible analysis |
| Descriptive Statistics & Visualization | 12 | Summarize distributions and communicate patterns responsibly |
| Probability & Distributions | 12 | Model uncertainty, random variables, distributions, and sampling behavior |
| Statistical Inference & Experimentation | 12 | Estimate parameters, test evidence, compare groups, and understand error |
| Correlation & Regression Modeling | 12 | Measure relationships, fit models, diagnose assumptions, and validate performance |
| Data Analytics & Business Statistics | 12 | Clean operational data and turn it into metrics, experiments, forecasts, and narratives |
| Data Science Statistics | 12 | Connect resampling, features, clustering, validation, Bayesian reasoning, and causality |
| Data Engineering Foundations | 12 | Understand analytical data formats, models, pipelines, quality, lineage, and semantic layers |
| Advanced Statistical Methods | 12 | Introduce experimental, survival, multivariate, simulation, Markov, and spatial methods |

## Difficulty distribution

- Beginner: 19 lessons
- Intermediate: 56 lessons
- Advanced: 33 lessons

## Learning formats

- Concept lessons: 75
- Lab-linked lessons: 22
- Practice-oriented lessons: 11

A format label indicates the dominant presentation style; every page still includes objectives, a core idea, application guidance, interpretation standards, common mistakes, related lessons, and completion controls.

## Career paths

### Data Analyst Path

Focuses on data foundations, descriptive statistics, cleaning, EDA, SQL, KPI design, cohorts, funnels, A/B testing, forecasting, regression, and communication.

### Data Scientist Path

Adds probability, inference, regression diagnostics, feature engineering, resampling, PCA, clustering, classification metrics, calibration, Bayesian inference, and causal reasoning.

### Data Engineer Path

Focuses on data representation, relational modeling, analytical SQL, normalization, ETL/ELT, batch and streaming concepts, warehouses/lakes/lakehouses, dimensional modeling, testing, orchestration, lineage, and semantic layers.

### Research & Business Decision Path

Focuses on sampling, measurement, uncertainty, experiments, comparisons, regression, forecasting, and defensible interpretation.

## Curriculum boundaries

The site teaches concepts and offers browser-sized demonstrations. It does not claim that a static browser lab replaces production data engineering systems, statistical software, peer-reviewed research design, or domain expertise.

Data engineering pages are conceptual learning pages rather than simulated production infrastructure. Advanced statistical pages introduce methods and assumptions without pretending that one short page provides complete professional mastery.
