Retail Sales Foundations Project

Audit a synthetic transaction dataset, define defensible metrics, summarize variation and write a short decision-ready finding.

Available now
01 · Download

Get the dataset and dictionary

The files are synthetic and contain no real customer or company data.

Download CSVDownload dictionary
02 · Frame

Define the analytical question

  • Which regions and channels produce the most revenue and profit?
  • How variable are daily sales?
  • Which products deserve further investigation?
03 · Audit

Check data quality before analysis

  1. GrainConfirm that one row represents one transaction line.
  2. TypesCheck dates, categories, integer quantities and numeric amounts.
  3. ReconciliationVerify profit equals revenue minus cost.
  4. DuplicatesConfirm transaction IDs are unique.
04 · Analyze

Produce a minimum evidence set

  • Total revenue, cost and profit
  • Average and median transaction revenue
  • Revenue and profit by region, channel and product
  • A histogram or box plot of transaction revenue
Summary labHistogram labBox plot lab
05 · Communicate

Write the final finding

Write three short sections: what happened, what evidence supports it, and what limitation or next check remains. Do not claim that a product or channel caused performance differences from this observational dataset alone.

Deliverables

  • Data-quality checklist
  • Metric table
  • Two useful charts
  • Three-paragraph finding
  • One limitation

Future reuse

This same dataset will return in Excel, SQL, Power BI and Python projects.
