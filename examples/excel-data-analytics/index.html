01

From vague request to workbook plan

A manager asks for “a sales dashboard.”

  1. Clarify the decision and reporting period.
  2. List required metrics and dimensions.
  3. Identify source files and quality checks.
  4. Plan separate Raw, Clean, Analysis, and Dashboard sheets.
  5. Define how the final numbers will be validated.

The workbook is a controlled analytical process, not merely a page of charts.
