Data and Statistics

Data are recorded observations; statistics provides methods for designing collection, summarizing variation, and drawing uncertainty-aware conclusions. Learn the difference and how they work together.

◷ 40 minBeginner

What you will learn

  • Define data and statistics separately.
  • Distinguish descriptive and inferential statistics.
  • Connect raw values to an analytical conclusion.
  • Recognize that data quality limits statistical conclusions.
01

What is data?

Data are recorded facts, labels, measurements, counts, text, images, or events about entities. A value becomes useful data when its meaning, unit, context, and source are known. “42” alone is ambiguous; “42 years, patient age at registration” is interpretable.

02

What is statistics?

Statistics is the discipline of learning from data. It includes planning data collection, describing data, measuring variation, estimating unknown quantities, testing claims, and communicating uncertainty. A calculation is statistical only when the data-generating context and interpretation are considered.

03

Descriptive and inferential statistics

Descriptive statistics summarize the observed data: counts, percentages, averages, spread, and charts. Inferential statistics use a sample to estimate or test something about a wider population, while accounting for sampling uncertainty.

Example

A dashboard showing last month’s recorded sales is descriptive. Estimating all customers’ satisfaction from a survey sample is inferential.

Vocabulary

Key terms

Data

Recorded values or observations with meaning and context.

Statistics

Methods and reasoning for collecting, summarizing, analyzing, and interpreting data.

Descriptive statistics

Methods that summarize the data actually observed.

Median salary of 80 employees.

Inferential statistics

Methods that use sample evidence to reason about a population.

Estimate voter support from a random sample.
Example

Seven days of retail sales

Daily sales in BDT: 12,000; 15,500; 11,800; 18,000; 16,200; 13,400; 21,000.

  1. 1

    The seven numbers are data values.

  2. 2

    Their unit is BDT and the entity is a day.

  3. 3

    The average, minimum, maximum, and chart are descriptive summaries.

  4. 4

    Using these seven days to predict every future day requires assumptions and uncertainty.

Conclusion

The observed average is about BDT 15,414; it describes these seven days, not a guaranteed future daily sale.
